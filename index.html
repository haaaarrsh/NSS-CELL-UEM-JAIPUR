National Service Scheme

About NSS (National Service Scheme)

Instilling the spirit of selfless voluntary community service in youth to foster nation-building, empathy, and social responsibility.

The Spirit of Youth Empowerment

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is an Indian government-sponsored flagship public service program conducted by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports of the Government of India. Popularly known as NSS, the scheme was launched in Gandhiji’s Centenary Year in 1969, aimed at developing student personality through community service.

NSS volunteers work with rural communities, villages, and slums to complete both 120 hours of regular service every year and special 7-day residential camps, addressing vital national priorities like health, hygiene, education, environment, and social integration.

NSS Motto: "NOT ME BUT YOU"

This motto reflects the essence of democratic living and upholds the need for selfless service. NSS teaches that the welfare of an individual depends on the welfare of society as a whole.

About UEM Jaipur (UEMJ)

University of Engineering & Management, Jaipur — Empowering young minds with cutting-edge academic excellence and socially committed leadership.

Excellence in Education, Values & Service

University of Engineering & Management (UEM), Jaipur is a premier autonomous university established in the year 2011 by Act No. 5 of 2012 of Govt. of Rajasthan. Located at the grand "GURUKUL" campus on the Jaipur-Sikar Highway, UEM Jaipur is accredited with prestigious national rankings, AICTE and UGC approvals, and NAAC accreditation.

Beyond academic rigor in Engineering, Management, Computer Applications, Physiotherapy, and Law, UEM Jaipur places deep priority on nation-building. Through its dedicated NSS Cell and Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) cell, the university actively mentors surrounding rural villages with free medical camps, literacy classes, clean drinking water projects, and solar energy installations.

University of Engineering & Management, Jaipur

Dedicated to the Nation: "Not Me But You"

The NSS Cell University of Engineering & Management, Jaipur (NSS CELL UEM JAIPUR) was established under the aegis of the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India, to provide young engineering, management, and healthcare students with direct field immersion in community welfare.

Our volunteers routinely spearhead mega blood donation drives, environmental afforestation programs, village adoption under Unnat Bharat Abhiyan, digital literacy bootcamps, and health diagnostic camps. We believe that true engineering intellect is realized when applied to solving pressing societal challenges of our villages and urban neighborhoods.
